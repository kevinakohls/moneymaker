How OEE is measured here. Availability and Quality are assumed to be 100%, so OEE = Performance = units the station actually made ÷ its theoretical average daily roll. What is Throughput Accounting?

Throughput Accounting judges decisions by their effect on the whole system. Throughput is the money a sale brings in. Here that is $400 per unit shipped. Operating Expense is the cost of running the factory each day, whether or not it ships much. Net profit = Throughput − Operating Expense − Investment.

Customers will buy everything you ship, so the way to make more money is to ship more, not to cut people. An improvement pays off only if it raises what the whole factory ships, and that depends on the constraint.

HOW TO PLAY

Make more money, not just more parts.

  • Five machines work in a line. Each day, each one rolls hidden dice to decide how much it could make. It can only work on material that was waiting for it at the start of the day.
  • Press Advance 1 Day or Advance 7 Days and watch the crates. Watch where crates pile up.
  • Tap a machine, then choose Raise Minimum (better bad days) or Raise Maximum (better good days). Each improvement costs $1,000.
  • Ship at least 85 units and finish day 20 with as much net profit as you can. Customers will buy everything you ship.
  • You can keep playing after day 20, but only day 20 counts toward your record.
