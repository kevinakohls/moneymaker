What is Throughput Accounting?

Throughput Accounting judges decisions by their effect on the whole system. Throughput is the money a sale brings in. Here that is $400 per unit shipped. Operating Expense is the cost of running the factory each day, whether or not it ships much. Net profit = Throughput − Operating Expense − Investment.

Customers will buy everything you ship, so the way to make more money is to ship more, not to cut people. An improvement pays off only if it raises what the whole factory ships, and that depends on the constraint.

HOW TO PLAY

Make more money, not just more parts.

  • Five machines work in a line. Each workday, each one rolls hidden dice to decide how much it could make. It can only work on material that was waiting for it at the start of the day.
  • A week is five workdays. Press Advance 1 Week or Advance 1 Month and watch the crates. Watch where crates pile up. You get a report at the end of each week.
  • Before you start, choose the one report you will manage by: average throughput, standalone throughput (SAT) or OEE. It stays locked for the whole game.
  • Tap a machine, then choose Raise Minimum (better bad days) or Raise Maximum (better good days). Each improvement costs $3,500 and adds 1 unit to that end of its daily dice.
  • Ship at least 545 units in six months (24 weeks) and finish with as much net profit as you can. Customers will buy everything you ship.
  • You can keep playing after week 24, but only week 24 counts toward your record.
